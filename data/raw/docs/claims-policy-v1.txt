DRAVIX HEALTH - CLAIMS & WAITING PERIOD POLICY
Version 1.0   Effective date: 15/01/2024   Status: superseded by version 2.0

Waiting periods
Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start, except accidents.
Pre-existing diseases: covered after 48 months of continuous coverage.
Specific illnesses (cataract, hernia, joint replacement, kidney stones): covered after 24 months.

Claim intimation
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated 48 hours before admission. Emergency hospitalisation must be intimated within 24 hours of admission.
