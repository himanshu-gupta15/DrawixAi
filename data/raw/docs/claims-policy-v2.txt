DRAVIX HEALTH - CLAIMS & WAITING PERIOD POLICY
Version 2.0   Effective date: 01-04-2026   Status: current

Waiting periods
Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start, except accidents which are covered from day one.
Pre-existing diseases: covered after 36 months of continuous coverage.
Specific illnesses (cataract, hernia, joint replacement, kidney stones): covered after 24 months.
Maternity (Gold and Platinum only): covered after 24 months.

Claim intimation
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated 48 hours before admission. Emergency hospitalisation must be intimated within 24 hours of admission through the app, the toll-free number 1800-200-3344 or the hospital insurance desk.

Claim settlement
Cashless pre-authorisation decisions are targeted within 1 hour. Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receiving complete documents. If a claim is rejected, the customer receives the reason in writing and can request a review within 30 days.

Network
Cashless treatment is available at more than 9,500 network hospitals in India. The current hospital list is available in the app.
