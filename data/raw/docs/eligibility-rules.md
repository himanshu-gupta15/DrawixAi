# Dravix Health – Underwriting & Eligibility Rules
Version: 2.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: Underwriting team | INTERNAL – Sales use

## Proposer eligibility
The proposer must be an Indian resident aged between 18 and 65 years (completed age) at entry. Proposers above 65 years are not eligible for a new policy and must be referred to a human advisor for senior-citizen options.

## Family members
A family floater can cover the proposer, spouse, up to 3 dependent children aged 91 days to 25 years, and parents. Parents can be added only under Gold or Platinum, with a maximum entry age of 75 years. Members who are 61 years or older at entry have a 20% co-payment on every claim.

## Pre-existing disease (PED)
Pre-existing diseases must be declared on the proposal form. Declared PED such as diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders and asthma is accepted and covered after a waiting period of 36 months. Non-disclosure of a PED can lead to claim rejection or policy cancellation.

## Conditions that need underwriter review
The following cannot be approved by sales and must be referred to an underwriter: any cancer diagnosis or treatment within the last 5 years, kidney failure or dialysis, heart surgery or angioplasty within the last 2 years, and any organ transplant. Sales agents must not promise approval in these cases.

## Medical check-up
Members aged 46 years and above require a pre-policy medical check-up arranged at home free of cost. Smokers and tobacco users pay a 10% premium loading.

## Lead qualification (sales)
A lead is qualified when: the proposer is 18–65, all members are within entry-age limits, the customer has consented to be contacted, and the expected annual premium budget is at least Rs 6,000. Leads with conditions needing underwriter review are marked "needs underwriting", not rejected.
