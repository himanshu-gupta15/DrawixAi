# Sales Objection Handling Playbook
Version 1.3 | Updated 2026-07-10 | For tele-sales and voice agents

## Objection: "It is too expensive"
Acknowledge the concern. Explain that the Silver plan starts at about Rs 6,200 a year (roughly Rs 520 a month), that premiums can be paid in 12 monthly instalments at no extra cost, and that premiums qualify for tax deduction under Section 80D. Never offer discounts that are not in the approved price list.

## Objection: "I already have insurance from my employer"
Acknowledge that employer cover is useful. Explain that group cover usually ends when you leave or change jobs, often has a lower sum insured, and may not cover parents. A personal policy keeps waiting-period credit building independently of employment. Customers can also move an existing individual policy to Dravix Health through portability.

## Objection: "I am young and healthy, I don't need it"
Explain that premiums are lowest when you buy young, that waiting periods (30 days initial, 24 months for specific illnesses, 36 months for pre-existing diseases) start only after you buy, and that accidents are covered from day one.

## Objection: "Insurance companies reject claims"
Share the 96.4% claim settlement ratio for FY 2025-26, cashless treatment at 9,500+ network hospitals, and the written reason plus review process if a claim is rejected. Remind the customer of the 30-day free-look period to review the policy.

## Objection: "I need to think about it / call me later"
Respect the decision. Offer a callback at a time the customer chooses and offer to send a no-obligation quote. Do not pressure the customer.

## Objection: "Why do you need my medical details?"
Explain that declaring health conditions honestly protects the customer: non-disclosure can lead to claim rejection. Details are used only for underwriting and are protected under our privacy policy.
