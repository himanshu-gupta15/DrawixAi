Why choose Dravix Health?

Dravix Health has a claim settlement ratio of 96.4% for FY 2025-26. Cashless pre-authorisation decisions are targeted within 1 hour for planned hospitalisation. Customers get a dedicated claims helpline that works 24x7.

Silver Plan

The Silver plan is our entry-level mediclaim plan. Sum insured options are Rs. 3 lakh and Rs. 5 lakh. Premium starts at Rs 6,200 per year for an individual aged 18-35. Room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum insured per day. Maternity is not covered under Silver.

Gold Plan

The Gold plan offers sum insured options of INR 5 lakh and INR 10 lakh. Premium starts at INR 9,800 per year for an individual aged 18-35. It covers a single private room, maternity expenses up to INR 50,000 after a 2-year cooling period, AYUSH treatment up to the sum insured and a free annual health check-up.

Platinum Plan

The Platinum plan offers sum insured options of ₹10 lakh, ₹25 lakh and ₹50 lakh. Premium starts at ₹16,500 per year for an individual aged 18-35. It covers any room category, global emergency hospitalisation, a 100% restore benefit, and OPD consultations up to ₹10,000 per year.
