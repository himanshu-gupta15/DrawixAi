Frequently Asked Questions

What is the waiting period for pre existing illness?

Pre existing illness (PED) declared at the time of buying the policy is covered after a waiting period of 4 years.

Is there a waiting period when I first buy the policy?

Yes. There is an initial cooling period of 30 days from the policy start date. Hospitalisation due to an accident is covered from day one.

How do I make a cashless claim?

Show your Dravix Health e-card at the insurance desk of any network hospital. The hospital sends a pre-authorisation request and we confirm the cashless approval, usually within 1 hour.

How do I claim cashless treatment?

Show your Dravix Health e-card at the insurance desk of any network hospital. The hospital sends a pre-authorisation request and we confirm cashless approval, typically within 1 hour.

How do I get a reimbursement claim?

If you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the bill and submit the claim form, discharge summary, original bills and investigation reports within 30 days of discharge. Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receiving all documents.

Can I get tax benefits?

Yes. Premiums paid are eligible for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act – up to Rs. 25,000 per year for self and family, and an additional Rs. 50,000 if you pay for senior-citizen parents. Tax benefits are subject to changes in tax law.

Can I pay the premium monthly?

Yes. You can choose annual payment or a monthly instalment option (12 EMIs) using auto-debit. There is no extra charge for monthly payment.

What is the grace period if I miss a payment?

Annual premiums have a grace period of 30 days; monthly instalments have a grace period of 15 days. Claims are not payable for hospitalisation that starts during the unpaid grace period.

Can I port my existing policy to Dravix Health?

Yes. Under portability, the waiting periods you have already completed with your previous insurer are credited, up to the sum insured of the previous policy. Apply at least 45 days before your current policy renewal date.

What is the free-look period?

You have 30 days from receiving the policy document to review it. If you cancel during the free-look period, the premium is refunded after deducting stamp duty and any medical check-up costs.

What is not covered?

Common exclusions are cosmetic or aesthetic surgery, self-inflicted injury, injuries from hazardous adventure sports, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, and treatment outside India (except Platinum global emergency cover).

Do I need a medical test?

Members aged 46 and above need a pre-policy medical check-up, which Dravix Health arranges at home free of cost. Members aged 45 or below generally do not need a test unless they declare a pre-existing disease.
